# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
General public (*masyarakat umum*) looking for transparency and updates regarding Bawaslu Kebumen's activities and records.

## Product Purpose
A living institutional journal and content calendar. It serves as a read-only public archive that maintains a chronological and structured record of the institution's activities.

## Positioning
A trustworthy and reliable source for Bawaslu Kebumen's official actions.

## Brand Personality
Modern, authoritative, and clean public archive.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, dated, or dense bureaucratic portals
- Flashy or overly casual social media feeds
- Unstructured, difficult-to-navigate data dumps

## Design Principles
- **Transparency first**: Content should be immediately legible, structured, and easy to consume.
- **Institutional but modern**: Build trust through a clean, polished presentation that avoids legacy government portal tropes.
- **Living history**: The interface should feel like an active, chronological journal rather than a static document repository.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high baseline readability and contrast for the general public, while maintaining a creative, modern, and engaging aesthetic.
